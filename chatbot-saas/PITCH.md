## Executive Summary

ChatBot Pro is a white-label AI chatbot platform that enables any business to deploy an intelligent customer engagement assistant in minutes — without writing code, building a website, or hiring developers.

Businesses get a fully branded chatbot that captures leads, answers questions, takes bookings, and accepts payments — available on their website, WhatsApp, or a dedicated standalone page.

## The Solution

A single platform that provides:

### 1. AI Chat Widget
- Embed on any website with **one line of code**
- Custom-branded colors, position, and greeting
- Collects name, email, phone, service interest
- Available 24/7 — never misses a lead

### 2. Standalone Business Page
- Auto-generated page: `yoursite.com/b/your-business`
- Share on Google Business, social media, flyers, QR codes
- No website needed — the page IS the digital storefront

### 3. Payment Collection
- Accept deposits and full payments via Paystack
- Generate payment links directly in conversation
- Automatic receipt and confirmation

### 4. Appointment Booking
- Clients select services, time slots, and dates
- Automated confirmations and reminders
- Syncs with Google Calendar (coming soon)

### 5. Lead Management Dashboard
- Real-time lead feed with status tracking
- Filter by service, source, date
- Export to CSV for CRM integration
- Email notifications on every new lead

### 6. WhatsApp Integration
- Same chatbot logic on WhatsApp Business API
- Reach customers where they already are
- Automated replies and conversation flows
